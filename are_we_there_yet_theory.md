**Abstract**  
Standard physicalist frameworks encounter persistent epistemological challenges in explaining how macroscopic subjective experience (qualia) emerges from classical electrochemical interactions ("the hard problem"). This paper articulates a non-emergentist alternative: the **Cross-Dimensional Beam Hypothesis (CDBH)**. We model universal consciousness not as an epiphenomenon generated by localized neurobiology, but as an invariant, scale-invariant field existing within a higher-dimensional manifold ($D \ge 4+1$). Individual subjective minds are modeled mathematically as local sections of a non-trivial fiber bundle, explaining the phenomenological illusion of isolated individuality as a geometric consequence of dimensional reduction.
